## 1. North Star Metric

**Monthly Delivered Revenue per Active Customer** — established in Phase 6 as the single metric this project treats as the clearest proxy for sustainable business health, because it ties revenue growth directly to customer value rather than treating revenue growth and customer growth as separate stories. It is the metric the Phase 10 What-If "Customer Value Impact" scenario is built directly around, and the metric every scenario lever in that page is ultimately measured against.

## 4. Why These Specific KPIs, and Not Others

Every KPI listed here was chosen because it is either (a) directly actionable — a number a decision-maker can point to and say "this is the lever we should pull," or (b) load-bearing for a lever tested in Phase 10's What-If scenarios (Conversion Rate, Repeat Purchase Rate, and AOV are the three parameters that page lets a decision-maker adjust). Vanity metrics that don't clearly connect to a decision — such as raw session counts with no conversion context — were deliberately left out of this top-level KPI set, even though the underlying data exists and is used elsewhere (see Phase 5/`06_Customer_Journey.md`).

## 5. Where These KPIs Live

These KPIs are surfaced live on the Executive, Sales, Customer, and Product Power BI pages (Phase 9), and form the exact baseline that every Phase 10 What-If scenario calculation is measured against — meaning the numbers in this document are not a static snapshot, but the same figures a viewer sees update in real time when interacting with either Power BI phase of this project.
